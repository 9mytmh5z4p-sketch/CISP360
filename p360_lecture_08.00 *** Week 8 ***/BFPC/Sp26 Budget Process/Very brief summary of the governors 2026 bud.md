#### Very Brief Summary of the Governors 2026 budget

**2.41% Cost-of-Living Adjustment (COLA)**. The Governor’s proposal includes $240.6 million for a 2.41% COLA for community college apportionments and approximately $29 million for various categorical programs. Critical for collective bargaining, but volatile because it's tied to state revenues.

**Strong Workforce and Student Equity and Achievement (SEA(P)) are NOT receiving funding increases from the categorical COLA**. [Source](https://www.socccd.edu/news/governors-2026-27-budget#:~:text=%2429%20million%20(2.41%25)%20cost,thousand%20increase%20to%20our%20programs.)

**Forecasted Small Enrollment Growth**. The budget allocates $87.2 million to cover a 1.5% increase in systemwide enrollment over two years. This includes $55.3 million for 1.0% growth in 2025-26 and $31.9 million for 0.5% growth in 2026-27. Notice: this is spread over two years and who knows what the fiscal picture is going to be like next year. [Source](https://ebudget.ca.gov/home). Los Rios expects slight enrollment growth for next year. The State expects K12 and Community College enrollments to diverge in the future. That could lead to difficult budget negotiations.

**$408.4 Million Deferral Repayment.** The Governor proposes to fully repay the $408.4 million in payment deferrals carried over from the 2025–26 budget. This means districts will receive cash that was already “earned” but not yet paid. It does not represent new TCR — it is the settlement of an outstanding liability. Bottom line for faculty: the repayment is good news for district fiscal stability, but it should not be confused with new money available for investment in people or programs.
Source: [LAO — 2026–27 Higher Education Overview (Report 5112)](https://lao.ca.gov/Publications/Report/5112)

**Calculation**
Los Rios 2025-26 TCR[^1]: ~ $432.8 million
Statewide 2025-26 TCR[^2]: ~ $10,011 million 
Los Rios' share: $432.8M ÷ $10,011M ≈ 4.3%
4.3% × $408.4M ≈ ~$17.5 million
Therefore Los Rios should expect to see ~$17 - $18 million.

**One-Time Deferred Maintenance Funding**. The Governor’s Budget proposes $120.7 million for deferred maintenance and special repairs of facilities. Note: ARC is due to receive $56.7 million in Proposition 2 funding for the Davies Hall replacement (not this $$).
Source: [California Community Colleges Capital Outlay Projects, EdBudget - Legislative Analyst's Office and the LRCCD Final Project Proposal (December 2024)](https://lao.ca.gov/Education/EdBudget/Details/974#:~:text=2025%2D26).

**Proposed $100mm Block Grant.** We should ask the administration what their plans are for FLC's portion of the one-time $100 million Student Support Block Grant. Los Rios will likely get ~$4.37mm.

**The New SCFF "Funding Floor" Protection**. The budget confirms the transition to a new revenue protection system where the district’s 2024-25 funding level serves as a permanent "floor". Districts will receive either their actual Student Centered Funding Formula (SCFF) generated amount or this 2024-25 floor amount, whichever is higher. Notably, this new floor does not grow with cumulative COLAs, unlike previous "hold harmless" provisions.
Why this is important to Faculty: This structural change explains why Los Rios may not see "new" money from enrollment growth. Growth in FTES simply helps the district’s actual performance "catch up" to the floor amount they are already receiving. For faculty, this means that while enrollment growth is vital for the long-term health of the college, it may not immediately translate into expanded discretionary resources or new positions until the district’s performance metrics exceed the established 2024-25 funding level.


Final Note: We still have the May Revise coming and who knows what the final budget figures are going to look like.
